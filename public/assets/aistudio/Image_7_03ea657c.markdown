## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-performance, immersive learning atmosphere tailored for ambition-driven professionals and modern knowledge seekers. Under the ethos "Learn beyond limits," the interface projects precision, cognitive clarity, and prestige. 

The aesthetic is Modern Tech Minimalist infused with disciplined Cyber-Refinement. Deep void-like backdrops eliminate ambient optical noise, focusing focus entirely onto rich educational content, code editors, high-definition streams, and interactive progress pathways. Visual differentiation is achieved through calculated luminous accents, crisp structural glass containers, and neon-violet focus cues that simulate elite production tooling rather than traditional static classrooms.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around pure visual hierarchy on deep canvas planes:
- **Base Canvas & Slate Hierarchy:** Root surface is `#0B0F19` (Obsidian Navy). Secondary elevations transition through structured slates: `#111827` (Surface Elevated), `#1E293B` (Surface Card), and `#334155` (Borders/Dividers).
- **Core Signal (Primary):** `#3B82F6` delivers high-energy clarity across active states, course progression bars, and primary CTAs, with `#2563EB` reserved for tactile press depth.
- **Neon Accent (Secondary):** `#8B5CF6` and `#A855F7` serve exclusively for achievements, streak counters, premium masterclasses, and specialized interactive feedback points.
- **Text & Contrast (Neutrals):** Pristine readability is anchored by `#FFFFFF` for primary titles, `#F8FAFC` for primary body copy, `#94A3B8` for secondary meta-information, and `#64748B` for subdued placeholder text.
- **Semantic Feedback:** Success states lean toward emerald (`#10B981`), critical warnings toward amber (`#F59E0B`), and errors toward crimson (`#EF4444`), all treated with matching dark luminous glows.

## Typography

The typographical architecture unites geometric velocity with structural legibility:
- **Headlines & Editorial Impact (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its forward-leaning curves and crisp geometric terminals. Applied across large marketing claims, course module titling, and dashboard metrics. Negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) maintains a tight, bespoke editorial feel.
- **Reading & Functional Systems (Inter):** Deployed for long-form lesson modules, transcripts, interactive quizzes, and technical documentation. Tall x-height and neutral grotesque forms prevent cognitive fatigue during multi-hour learning sessions.
- **Labels & Microcopy (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Scaled at medium to extra-bold weights with loose tracking to retain legibility across glassmorphic badges, runtime indicators, progress tags, and code lesson step counters.

## Layout & Spacing

The system operates on an 8pt base grid with a 12-column responsive fluid grid:
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with `2rem` gutters and a maximum content constraint of `1440px`. Safe margins are clamped at `3rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `1.5rem` outer margins. Sidebars collapse to responsive draw-overs or persistent compact icon rails.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` page edge margins. Stack horizontal card tracks into native-feeling touch carousels with edge-bleed padding.
- **Spacing Principle:** Micro-elements (icon-to-label, badge padding) leverage `space-xs` and `space-sm`. Structural cards and syllabus feeds deploy `space-md` internally and `space-lg` to `space-xl` for sectional breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through luminous containment, tonal layering, and directional glass optics:
- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#0B0F19`. Zero elevation.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Module Shells):** `#111827` elevated background wrapped with a crisp low-contrast border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Layer 2 (Floating Overlays & Modals):** Translucent `#1E293B` set to 75% opacity with a `24px` backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`) and a hairline top rim highlight: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`.
- **Glow & Interactive Feedback:** Hover and active layers discard opaque dropshadows in favor of tinted optical diffusion. Primary active components project `0px 8px 30px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.25)`. High-tier achievements and streaks radiate subtle violet luminescence via `0px 8px 30px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a pill-forward and continuous-curve morphology to offset the technical rigidity of code blocks and analytics:
- **Pill Philosophy (Level 3):** Buttons, interactive tags, category chips, status badges, and search bars utilize a full pill radius (`9999px` / `rounded-full`).
- **Container Curvature:** Large structural shells, video player wraps, and code inspection cards inherit `rounded-xl` (1.5rem to 2rem) corner geometry to establish comfortable visual cradles for complex lesson assets.
- **Precision Nested Radii:** Child elements inside cards must use proportionally smaller radii (outer radius minus internal padding) to preserve geometric concentricity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Pill-shaped, background `#3B82F6`, text `#FFFFFF`, transition 200ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1). On hover: background `#2563EB`, shadow `0 0 24px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.45)`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Translucent slate (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.7)`), 1px hairline border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`), text `#F8FAFC`. On hover: border `rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.5)`, background `rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.1)`.
- **Achievement / Premium Action:** Pill-shaped, linear gradient `135deg, #8B5CF6, #3B82F6`, shadow `0 0 20px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.35)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Glassmorphic Badge:** Full pill geometry. Fill: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`, backdrop-blur: `12px`, border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Typography: `label-sm` uppercase.
- **Topic Filter Chip:** Pill geometry with clickable feedback. Unselected: dark slate fill with `#94A3B8` text. Selected: electric blue glow, solid `#3B82F6` text fill, border `rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.6)`.

### Lists & Syllabus Trees
- Modules utilize sleek, semi-transparent row containers with subtle divider lines (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`). Active modules reveal an electric blue vertical indicator bar (3px width, full border radius) pinned to the far left.

### Form Inputs & Search
- Surface: `#111827`, border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Border-radius: pill (`9999px`) for quick search, `rounded-lg` for multi-field lesson submissions.
- Focused state: border shifts to `#3B82F6` accompanied by an ambient glow ring: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 4px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom circular and squircle geometries (`rounded-md` for checks, `rounded-full` for radios).
- Inactive: dark surface with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`. Active: filled `#3B82F6` with bright white micro-indicators and a subtle `#3B82F6` external bloom.

### Content & Course Cards
- Construction: `rounded-xl` surface containers in `#111827` featuring dynamic top rim lighting (`linear-gradient(90deg, rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.3), rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.3), transparent)`).
- Hover state: 4px vertical transform lift (`translateY(-4px)`), outer glow intensification, and dynamic zoom transition on preview thumbnails.

### Specialized EdTech Components
- **Lesson Progress Bar:** Sleek 4px track (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) with a glowing electric-blue-to-violet linear fill and trailing neon pulse head.
- **Interactive Code Terminal / Sandbox:** Obsidian deep background (`#070A10`), hairline borders, monospaced line numbering in `#64748B`, with glowing syntax tokens aligned with system violet and blue palettes.
